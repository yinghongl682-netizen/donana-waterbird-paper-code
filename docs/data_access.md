# External data access

Monitoring observations are provided through the official ICTS-Doñana/EBD-CSIC aerial and terrestrial census services listed in `data/sources.yml`. Obtain applicable notices, exact exports and lawful access separately. This release neither redistributes those records nor promises a publicly obtainable byte-identical frozen input pack.

The external pack requires `raw_aerial.zip`, monthly `raw_ground/CT_YYYYMM/` native opportunities/count tables and original notices, taxon/reporting/water/native hierarchy contracts, locked protocols and authorized frozen validation oracles. The qualified study intake and eligibility rules are not inferred from a complete calendar. Preserve absence/missingness separately from supported zero.

Fig. 1 additionally requires externally supplied map and satellite-window inputs with the exact contracts in `data/schema/`. Neither public display of a manuscript map nor MIT licensing of its renderer authorizes redistribution of its inputs. No observation names, free text, tracks, native records, geodata or font binaries are included in this code release.
